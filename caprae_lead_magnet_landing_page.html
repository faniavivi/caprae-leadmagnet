What the checklist covers

Before making a major decision about the future of a company, it helps to have a clear view of where the business stands today.

Business Performance

Review revenue, profitability, customers, and the current position of the business.

Financial Position

Take a closer look at the financial information a potential investor or buyer may want to understand.

Operations & Team

Consider how the company operates and how dependent it is on the owner.

Market Position

Review customers, competitors, market position, and the areas where the company stands out.

Growth Opportunities

Identify areas where the company could expand, improve, or develop new opportunities.

Exit Readiness

Consider what may need to be prepared if you decide to explore a future sale or investment.

Why use this checklist?

It is designed to give business owners a simple starting point before speaking with investors, advisors, or potential buyers.
